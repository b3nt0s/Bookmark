lightbulbHow it works

From a paper flyer to a calendar event in three steps

Bookmark reads the event details off a photo of a flyer, so you don't have to type them in yourself.

  1. 01 photo_camera

    Snap or upload

    Take a photo of a poster, handout or bulletin board, or upload one you already have.

  2. 02 neurology

    AI reads the details

    An AI model reads the text on the flyer and pulls out what you need for the event.

  3. 03 event_available

    Review and add

    Check the title, date, time and place, fix anything, then add it to Google Calendar or download it as a calendar file.

tips_and_updatesTips for a good scan

  • Fit the whole flyer in the photo, straight on.
  • Use good light and avoid glare on glossy paper.
  • One event per photo works best.
  • JPG, PNG, WEBP and iPhone (HEIC) photos up to 10 MB.

verified_userWhat happens to your photo

  • It's sent to Bookmark only to read its text, and isn't saved on our server.
  • Events you add are listed under Recent Events, saved in this browser.
  • You sign in with Google so Bookmark knows whose calendar it's for.

Questions

What if it reads something wrong?expand_more

Every detail is editable on the review screen before you add the event, so you can fix any mistake first.

Can I use it on my phone?expand_more

Yes. On a phone, "take a photo" opens your camera so you can scan a flyer right where you see it.

Where do I find events I already added?expand_more

Open Recent Events. From there you can open an event in Google Calendar again, download it, share it, or remove it from the list.
